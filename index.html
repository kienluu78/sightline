Glasses POV
when you ask.

An iOS client on Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit 0.8.0. The glasses are the camera. The phone is the screen. The stream stays off until you ask, then it closes when you are done. Built to share a sharp landscape feed, not to leave High running for half an hour, and not to ship Meta’s sample chrome as a product.

What it is

  • An iOS client for Meta Ray-Ban DAT: connect, wait for an active device, stream, photo.
  • On demand: say Sightline to open the glasses POV. Say stop to close it. Proven across several cycles in one sitting, including while a live voice share was already running.
  • LZRC chrome: cream, navy, teal. Springboard name Sightline. Mark is the LZRC monogram.
  • Landscape High (720x1280) for a full-width share. Portrait uses fit so the whole frame stays visible.
  • Field-tested outdoors. Stream held. About one second of delay while live.

What it is not

  • Not Intent-Lab VisionClaw. Different tree. No Gemini Live in the field HUD.
  • Not a rebrand of Meta’s CameraAccess sample as an LZRC product. DAT stays under Meta Wearables Developer Terms.
  • Not DAT 0.9.0. The iOS package is pinned 0.8.0. Glasses firmware may already show a 0.9.x companion. That split is honest.
  • Not a public App Store binary. A store build waits on Meta’s Wearables path. We do not publish the iOS client as source in the meantime. KeyGraph remains the memory idea. Not clinical decision support.

What we actually changed

Registration, DAT session, and the glasses pipe stay Meta’s. We changed the operator surface: Home, waiting, stream HUD, icon, display name, and an on-demand listen layer so the camera is a request, not a pipe. Resolution still locks when the session starts. High is a badge while live, not a mid-stream switch.

Field notes

  • Always-on High is the wrong default. It drains. A share only needs the frame when you speak.
  • High is usable on Wi-Fi. High can pause on cellular. Low stayed stable outdoors.
  • Long outdoor runs can drop the DAT link. Reconnect is still Meta AI: Disconnect, then Connect my glasses. There is no custom reconnect daemon.
  • DAT 0.9.0 for iOS is a later sitting. It is an API change (addCamera), not a promised fix for heat or cellular High.
